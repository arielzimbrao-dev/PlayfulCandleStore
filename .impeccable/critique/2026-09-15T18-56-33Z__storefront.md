## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Committed, distinctive retro-groovy identity (the palette is genuinely used; wave dividers, groovy ribbon, VIP swirl, floating maple leaves, sticker badges give real character). NOT immediately "AI made this" — but two tells drag it there: (1) a tiny Unbounded eyebrow above *every* section/page (textbook AI section-grammar), and (2) the register-killer — product photos rendered as a giant pale-pink card with a tiny 🕯️ emoji across Loja/category/collection/rails/PDP ("colored blocks where a hero photo belongs"). For a candle boutique the photo IS the pitch, so these read as broken/unfinished.

**Deterministic scan (detector):** TSX/JSX markup CLEAN (0 findings). `styles/global.scss` → 2 `layout-transition` warnings (genuine, low-impact): `.car-dots .dot` (line 220, animates `width`) and `.ship-bar__track span` (line 438, progress bar width). No false positives.

**Deterministic contrast (getComputedStyle + WCAG):**
- **`.btn--primary` primary CTA — white on coral rgb(255,77,125) = 3.18:1 — FAIL (need 4.5).** Systemic; it's the main "Adicionar ao carrinho" / "Ver a loja" button.
- `.buybox__stock` "Em stock" green on pink = 3.02:1 — FAIL.
- `.eyebrow` pink-deep on pink = 4.40:1 — near-miss FAIL. Same for "Ver detalhes de envio" link.
- Body/ink/facet/footer/gold-on-dark text all PASS. `--ink-soft` on `--paper` ≈ 5.8:1 (passes) — muted body text is fine.
- White `.chero__text` / gold eyebrow over the hero PHOTO rely on the 48% scrim — not statically computable; marginal over bright image regions.

**Mobile 375px:** no horizontal scroll on `/` or `/produtos` (measured). Tap targets: header icon buttons under 44px — `.burger` 39×38, `.hd-icon` search/account/lang/cart 40×40 (near-miss). Text-link "under 44px" counts are line-height artifacts, not real failures.

## Overall Impression
A genuinely charming, committed brand identity that the home page delivers beautifully — undercut at the exact wrong place: the highest-intent screens (product grid + PDP) show the *least* imagery on the site, as empty pink placeholders. Fix the imagery + the AA contrast on the primary CTA and this jumps from "Good" to "Excellent".

## What's Working
1. **Committed retro-groovy identity** — vibrant palette actually used, distinctive motifs (waves, ribbon, VIP swirl, maple leaves, stickers).
2. **Mobile-first hero** — full-bleed photo, gold-accent headline, dual CTA, trust row, stronger mobile scrim for legibility.
3. **Real a11y + conversion scaffolding** — cart drawer focus-trap/Escape/restore, skip link, focus-visible rings, reduced-motion handling, PDP trust/stock signals.

## Priority Issues

- **[P0] Emoji placeholders where product photos belong** (`ProductCardBig` `.bcard__ph`, `ProductGallery` `.gallery__ph`). The product photo is the whole pitch; empty pink grids kill desire + trust at peak intent. Fix: require real `featuredImage`; interim, a full-bleed branded placeholder (type-colored ground + product name) so a card never looks empty.
- **[P1] Primary CTA fails AA contrast (3.18:1).** White on coral. Fix: darken the button fill to coral-deep (white passes ≈4.9:1).
- **[P1] Scroll-reveal blank voids** — sections at opacity:0 until they cross the threshold + tall padding → 1+ viewport of empty pink between sections; observer/JS failure risks blank content. Fix: reveal earlier (rootMargin/threshold), reduce mobile padding, guarantee visible fallback.
- **[P1] Eyebrow above every section/page** — the #1 AI-slop grammar. Fix: keep only informational eyebrows, drop the decorative reflex.
- **[P2] Mobile filter wall** — ~14 filter controls + sort before the first product at 375px. Fix: collapse filters into a "Filtros" drawer/accordion on mobile.
- **[P2] One hero photo reused 5+ times** (ig1.jpg). Fix: distinct image per category/collection.
- **[P3] Inner-page polish gap + low-contrast bits** — plain shop/PDP/cart vs lush home; empty cart bland; cart/drawer lack trust badges; `.buybox__stock` + eyebrow near-miss contrast; cart badge shows "0" when empty.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Riley (stress):** `queimador-branco` PDP omits Detalhes accordion (no description); rapid filter changes = full nav, no loading state; free-ship €35 hardcoded in TWO files (CartDrawer + carrinho) with "align with Shopify" TODO → mismatch risk; drawer & cart duplicate discount/qty logic.

## Questions to Consider
1. If you deleted every eyebrow kicker, would any information be lost — or would hierarchy get stronger?
2. A candle store's pitch is "look how beautiful this is" — why does the highest-intent screen show the least imagery?
3. Home is lush, shop/cart are plain — which does a buyer spend 80% of their time on, and which did you finish first?
